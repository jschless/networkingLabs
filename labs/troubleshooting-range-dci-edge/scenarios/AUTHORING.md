[`Troubleshooting Range Scenario Contract`](../../troubleshooting-range/scenarios/AUTHORING.md).

`labs/troubleshooting-range/references/range-routing.md`, which is absent.

and address data, healthy paths, safe runtime mutations, and catalog uniqueness.

links, addressing, or the healthy architecture.
