# Ticket CR-301 — Meeting-room port went offline after a room change

**Reported by:** Facilities support
**Impact:** A meeting-room wall port stopped working immediately after a room layout change; other corporate desks remain online.
**Symptom:** The endpoint link indicator is lit, but the access switch reports the port unavailable. Facilities confirms that temporary room equipment was connected shortly before the failure.

Diagnose, fix, and verify the incident. Submit interface and event evidence, the minimal safe correction, and proof that the restored port forwards without weakening protection for normal user-facing ports.
