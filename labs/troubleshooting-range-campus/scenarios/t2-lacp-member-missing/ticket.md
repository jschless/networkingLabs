# Ticket CR-303 — Campus uplink redundancy is degraded

**Reported by:** Network monitoring
**Impact:** Users remain connected, but the campus access uplink has lost half of its designed redundant capacity.
**Symptom:** Both physical circuits show carrier. The logical uplink remains up, yet monitoring reports only one forwarding member where two are expected.

Diagnose, fix, and verify the incident. Submit bundle and member-state evidence, the minimal correction, restored two-member forwarding proof, and a user-path test that confirms service was not needlessly disrupted.
