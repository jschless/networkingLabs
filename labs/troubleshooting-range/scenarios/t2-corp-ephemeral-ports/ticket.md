# Ticket TR-208 — Corporate client cannot open new sessions

**Reported by:** Corporate desktop support
**Impact:** One corporate workstation cannot open new portal sessions, while
existing sessions remain connected and users on other networks are unaffected.
**Symptom:** Name resolution, ICMP reachability, and the remote portal are
healthy. New connection attempts from the affected workstation fail locally
before a TCP handshake appears on the network.

Diagnose, fix, and verify the incident. Submit endpoint socket and transport
evidence, the minimal correction, and tests of both new and existing sessions.
