# Golden TCP service and client expectations

services1 listens on 10.250.40.10:8080 (not loopback-only).
The idle listener has an empty receive queue and accepts new sessions.
Corporate clients use the Linux default ephemeral range 32768-60999.
New TCP/8080 connections succeed from both corporate and voice clients.
Repeated DNS, ICMP, and HTTP checks complete without intermittent loss.

Useful comparison commands:
  services1: ss -lntp '( sport = :8080 )'
  services1: ss -lnt '( sport = :8080 )'
  corp1:     sysctl net.ipv4.ip_local_port_range
  corp1:     ss -tan '( dport = :8080 )'
  corp1:     ping -c 10 10.250.40.10
